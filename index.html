Data and privacy

This first version saves data in this browser on this device. It does not require an account or send your study data to a server. Clearing browser site data can erase your records, so use Export my data regularly. No cross-device sync yet.

Created Siddharth Dixit

I created StudyOS because I wanted a single place where students could organise their studies, track their progress and manage their academic goals. I wanted to make studying more organised and less stressful. This project is my attempt to turn that idea into a working application.
